What About Shipping and Other Costs?

Factory prices exclude international shipping, insurance, import duties, taxes, customs clearance, and local delivery.

For initial budgeting, allow approximately 20–40% above the furniture cost for additional expenses. For example, RMB 100,000 in furniture could mean a preliminary budget of RMB 120,000–140,000.

This is a broad estimate, not a quote. Actual costs depend on your destination and shipping arrangements and may fall outside this range.
