Furniture Catalogs — Page Sitemap

URL: /
Page role: The main discovery and catalog resource for visitors researching residential furniture in Foshan.
Primary user need: Understand what types of quality furniture and manufacturers are available, then explore selected catalogs and showroom imagery.
Primary action: Browse useful catalog and showroom information.
Secondary action: Select "Need More Help?" to open the contact modal.

1. Site Header
Include:
* InsideFoshan wordmark or text-based logo.
* Furniture Catalogs
* Why Foshan
* How to Shop
* Need More Help?
Furniture Catalogs is the active page.
The header should be simple and compact. The help action should be noticeable without looking like an aggressive sales button.
On mobile, keep navigation easy to use without letting the header dominate the screen.

2. Introduction / Opening Section
Purpose
Tell visitors immediately what this page offers.
Content
A concise headline about discovering selected quality furniture in Foshan.
A short supporting paragraph explaining that InsideFoshan presents a curated selection of furniture manufacturers and useful catalogs for residential homeowners.
Make clear that the selection focuses on quality furniture, including designer-inspired styles.
Do not use a long brand manifesto or an extended explanation of Foshan.
Optional introductory distinction
Briefly orient visitors with two simple statements:
Furnishing an entire home?
Explore manufacturers with broad furniture collections across multiple rooms.
Looking for something specific?
Explore specialists focused on particular furniture and furnishing categories.
These statements may act as simple visual cues or links to the relevant sections lower on the same page.
They must not create additional pages or navigation levels.

3. Broad-Range Furniture Manufacturers
Purpose
Help visitors discover manufacturers whose collections cover multiple rooms and may support furnishing a substantial part of a home.
Initial content
Approximately five manufacturer profiles.
The actual manufacturer information and supplied assets will be added as available.
Section introduction
A short sentence explaining that these manufacturers offer broad residential furniture collections.
Manufacturer profile structure
Each profile should contain only the elements that add value:
1. Manufacturer reference or short heading
    * Use a descriptive heading or stable reference number.
    * Do not reveal the factory's public-facing identity.
2. Short description
    * One or two sentences explaining the range and distinctive qualities.
    * Communicate positioning naturally where relevant.
    * Avoid unsupported superlatives and formal price-tier badges.
3. Photography
    * One strong representative image where available.
    * An optional second image, such as an exterior and showroom interior.
    * Prefer authentic showroom and product photography.
4. Catalog links
    * Include only catalogs that meet the quality standard.
    * Group catalog links by clear product category.
    * Open or download PDFs directly when clicked.
    * Do not insert an intermediate preview page.
5. Optional showroom gallery
    * Use when several useful showroom photographs are available and a catalog is weak or unsuitable.
    * A simple carousel is acceptable when it adds value.
    * Do not require a carousel for every profile.
6. Optional assistance action
    * Provide a clear route to request an introduction or further help where appropriate.
Catalog quality rule
A profile does not need a catalog link to be included.
Prefer a strong selection of useful catalogs over an exhaustive collection of weak catalogs.
Avoid catalogs dominated by computer-generated renderings when better real-world material is available.

4. Specialist Furniture and Furnishing Manufacturers
Purpose
Help visitors find manufacturers focused on particular furniture or furnishing categories.
Initial categories
* Outdoor furniture
* Chairs
* Stone tables
* Carpets
* Mattresses
Additional categories, potentially including lighting and cabinetry, may be introduced later.
Do not design the page so rigidly that new categories are difficult to add.
Section introduction
A concise explanation that these manufacturers specialize in particular product types, helping visitors explore a specific need rather than a broad furniture collection.
Specialist profile structure
Use the same flexible profile principles as the broad-range section:
* Descriptive heading or reference number.
* Concise description of the specialty.
* One or two representative images where available.
* Selected, clearly categorized catalog links when useful.
* Optional showroom gallery when real photography provides more value.
* A route to further assistance where appropriate.
The emphasis should be on what the manufacturer specializes in, not on giving every profile identical content.

5. Curation and Selection Note
Purpose
Explain the scope and standards of the selection.
Key messages
* Featured manufacturers have been deliberately shortlisted for quality and relevance.
* The website focuses on residential furniture and related furnishings.
* It does not attempt to represent every manufacturer, product category, or price point in Foshan.
* The broader market includes a wide variety of manufacturers and business models.
* The selection will grow as additional worthwhile manufacturers are identified.
Keep this explanation short and factual.
Do not imply that the listed manufacturers represent the entirety of Foshan's market or that every factory is suitable for every buyer.
This section can appear before or after the manufacturer listings. Choose the placement that produces the clearest page flow.

6. Need More Help?
Purpose
Give visitors a low-pressure way to request assistance without turning the page into a sales funnel.
Content
A short heading and sentence inviting visitors to get in touch if they need help finding the right information or want to explore a factory introduction or sourcing assistance.
Interaction
Clicking the action opens the global contact modal.
The modal is specified separately in the master brief and does not require its own page.

7. Introductory-Fee Disclosure
Include a concise, unobtrusive disclosure explaining that an introductory fee may be received when connecting buyers with selected factories.
Suggested wording:
"We may receive an introductory fee when connecting buyers with selected factories."
Place it where visitors can reasonably find it without interrupting the main browsing experience.
Confirm the wording and placement before launch.

8. Site Footer
Include:
* Links to Furniture Catalogs, Why Foshan, and How to Shop.
* A secondary Need More Help? action.
* Any necessary legal or privacy links, once the relevant pages and policies exist.
Do not invent a physical address, company registration details, or contact information.

Content and Design Rules
* Keep the page flat and easy to scan.
* Keep broad-range and specialist manufacturers on the same page.
* Use clear product categories for catalog links.
* Prioritize catalog usefulness over the number of catalogs displayed.
* Prefer real showroom photography to renderings.
* Do not expose factory names or identifying details.
* Do not use Premium/Luxury badges in the initial version.
* Do not force every profile into an identical layout.
* Do not use automatic carousels, unnecessary animation, or heavy decorative effects.
* Ensure PDF links work directly and remain easy to update.
* Make the layout responsive and accessible.
Suggested Visual Hierarchy
1. Compact header.
2. Short, useful introduction.
3. Optional two-part discovery cue.
4. Broad-range manufacturers.
5. Specialist manufacturers.
6. Brief curation and scope note.
7. Need More Help?
8. Footer and disclosure.
This is a recommended starting order, not an inflexible rule. Adjust it if the implemented page reads more naturally with the curation note in a different position.
