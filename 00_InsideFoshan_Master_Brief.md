InsideFoshan.com — Master Website Brief

1. Project Overview
InsideFoshan.com is an independent, curated information resource for international homeowners exploring high-quality, designer-inspired furniture in Foshan, China.
The website helps visitors understand what is available in Foshan, discover selected furniture manufacturers, browse useful catalogs, and make better-informed decisions about exploring the market.
This is not intended to be a comprehensive directory of every furniture manufacturer in Foshan. It is a selective guide built around quality, relevance, and useful information.
The website should make it easier for visitors to navigate an unfamiliar furniture ecosystem without overwhelming them with unnecessary information.

2. Primary Audience
The primary audience is international residential homeowners who:
* Have ambitious plans for their homes.
* Are interested in high-quality, designer-inspired furniture.
* Are researching Foshan as a potential furniture-sourcing destination.
* Want to understand the kinds of products and manufacturers they can discover there.
* Value practical information, curated recommendations, and access to useful catalogs.
* May explore the market independently or seek assistance with sourcing.
The website should be useful to visitors whether or not they choose to use a sourcing service.

3. Primary Objective
The primary objective is to provide clear, digestible, genuinely useful information about discovering quality residential furniture in Foshan.
Prioritize:
1. Helping visitors understand what kinds of furniture and manufacturers they can find.
2. Giving visitors access to selected, useful catalogs and real showroom photography.
3. Making the information easy to navigate and understand.
4. Explaining relevant aspects of Foshan and the furniture-sourcing process.
5. Offering a discreet route for visitors who need further assistance.
Qualified enquiries are valuable, but this is fundamentally an information-led website rather than a conventional sales landing page.

4. Website Architecture
The website is plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. There is no Astro project and no node_modules folder. The file you edit is the page visitors see. GitHub Pages can serve these files directly.
The website has three principal content pages:
/ — Furniture Catalogs — index.html
/whyfoshan/ — Why Foshan — whyfoshan/index.html
/howtosource/ — How to Shop — howtosource/index.html
There is no separate homepage. The root URL is the main furniture discovery and catalog page.
Shared files:
* css/styles.css — layout and visual design
* css/fonts.css and fonts/ — IBM Plex Sans and Libre Baskerville
* js/site.js — menu, contact window, catalog window, and photo window
* images/ — showroom photographs
* catalogs/ — PDF files
A new section is a new folder with its own index.html, for example pricecalculator/index.html. Copy the header, footer, and contact window from an existing page, then add the new link to the navigation in each page.
A persistent or consistently accessible Need More Help? action opens a contact modal. It does not require a separate page.
Keep the website relatively flat. Do not create individual factory pages, category pages, or additional navigation levels for the initial version.
To add a factory or catalog, edit the matching block in index.html and put the PDF in catalogs/ and any new photograph in images/. Placeholder rows say “File to be added” or “Photographs to be added” until real files exist.

5. The Furniture Catalogs Page
The root page is the primary attraction.
It should feature a curated selection of approximately ten manufacturers initially, divided conceptually into:
* Broad-range furniture manufacturers that offer products for multiple rooms.
* Specialist manufacturers focused on particular furniture or furnishing categories.
These are sections within one page, not separate pages.
Potential specialist categories include outdoor furniture, chairs, stone tables, carpets, and mattresses. Additional categories, such as lighting and cabinetry, may be added over time.
Not every manufacturer must have a catalog link. Publish catalogs only when they meet the site's quality and usefulness standards.
Where useful, catalogs should be grouped by clear product categories. Clicking a catalog should open or download the PDF directly, without an intermediate preview page.
Real showroom photography is preferred to computer-generated product renderings.

6. Curation and Factory Identity
Only include manufacturers that meet the site's quality threshold.
The selection focuses on high-quality residential furniture, including premium and luxury offerings. It is not intended to represent the full range of Foshan's furniture market.
Describe manufacturers accurately and naturally. Avoid formal Premium or Luxury badges in the initial version. Where relevant, communicate product positioning through concise descriptions.
Do not display factory names or identifying details on the public website.
Factory introductions may be offered to legitimate, qualified buyers. Public listings should still provide meaningful information about the manufacturer's product range and strengths.
Catalogs and photographs must be used with appropriate permission. Do not assume that removing factory names or logos automatically grants permission to reproduce or distribute the underlying materials.

7. Commercial Transparency
InsideFoshan may receive an introductory fee when connecting buyers with selected factories.
Use a concise, unobtrusive disclosure consistent with the actual arrangement:
"We may receive an introductory fee when connecting buyers with selected factories."
The final wording and placement should be reviewed before launch to ensure that the disclosure is accurate and meets applicable requirements.
Do not portray the site as wholly independent of commercial relationships when such relationships are relevant to a recommendation or introduction.

8. Relationship to BeyondShowrooms.com
InsideFoshan.com and BeyondShowrooms.com serve related but distinct purposes.
InsideFoshan is the information and discovery resource.
BeyondShowrooms is the sourcing-service website.
InsideFoshan should not simply reproduce BeyondShowrooms' mission statement or become an overt sales funnel. Keep the focus on practical information about Foshan and its furniture ecosystem.
Visitors may choose to explore the market independently or ask for further assistance.

9. Content and Writing Principles
Use clear, direct, natural English.
Prioritize:
* Short paragraphs.
* Useful headings.
* Concise descriptions.
* Clear product categories.
* Practical explanations.
* Specific information over generic promotional language.
Avoid:
* Long introductory essays.
* Empty marketing claims.
* Unsupported claims about quality, value, or authenticity.
* Repetitive calls to action.
* Overexplaining information that visitors can understand quickly.
* Treating the website as a comprehensive encyclopedia of Foshan.
The writing should feel knowledgeable and helpful without sounding corporate or sales-driven.

10. Visual Direction
InsideFoshan should feel like an independent, practical information resource: clean, slightly raw, understated, and distinctive without being overdesigned.
The website should feel functional and approachable, not like a polished corporate website, luxury furniture brand, or conventional online marketplace.

Design Principles
Overall feel: Utilitarian, independent, slightly unconventional, with a subtle retro or industrial influence.
Colors: Warm neutral backgrounds, dark typography, and one distinctive accent color.
Typography: Strong, straightforward, and readable, with some character.
Layout: Simple grids, clear information hierarchy, and occasional asymmetry.
Imagery: Real furniture and showroom photography should be the main visual focus.
Graphic details: Use occasional numbers, labels, borders, and block-like elements to add personality without clutter.
Overall finish: Clean and coherent, but not glossy or excessively polished.
Keep the design simple and easy to navigate. Establish personality through typography, composition, color, and a few consistent graphic details rather than decorative effects.
Avoid generic corporate styling, luxury-brand polish, excessive animations, and fashionable design gimmicks. Raw should mean straightforward and unpretentious, not messy or difficult to use.
Visual References
Use these sites as inspiration for specific qualities, not as templates:
Yale School of Art — unconventional, independent character. https://www.art.yale.edu/
FORMHAUS — graphic structure and restrained color. https://superdesign.dev/library/formhaus-agency-website-brutalist-red
Livia Satriano — independent curation. https://liviasatriano.com/it/bio/
Lonneke van der Palen — clean, utilitarian simplicity. https://www.lonnekevanderpalen.com/
Guiding principle: Clean, useful, and distinctive, without looking overdesigned.

11. Photography and Catalogs
Prioritize authentic, high-quality photography of actual furniture and showrooms.
A manufacturer profile may include:
* One representative photograph.
* An optional second photograph, such as an exterior and an interior view.
* A showroom gallery or carousel when several photographs add genuine value.
* Direct links to selected PDF catalogs when those catalogs are useful.
Do not require every manufacturer to have the same number of images or catalog links.
Do not fill gaps with generic stock photography or invented factory imagery.
When actual project assets are unavailable, build the layout using clearly identified placeholders that can be replaced later.

12. Interaction and Contact
The Need More Help? action should be consistently accessible and open a contact modal.
The modal may eventually support different enquiry intentions, including:
* Requesting a factory introduction.
* Getting assistance with furniture sourcing.
For the first version, implement a straightforward, functional interaction. Do not invent a backend, email address, or submission service that has not been supplied.
If no working submission endpoint exists, provide a clear, configurable placeholder or an appropriately implemented fallback.

13. Technical and Content Principles
* Build a responsive website that works on desktop, tablet, and mobile.
* Use semantic HTML and accessible interaction patterns.
* Make navigation and catalog links easy to understand.
* Keep the site fast and lightweight.
* Keep the site as plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript so each page is visible in the folder.
* Use real supplied assets when available.
* Do not invent factory names, endorsements, statistics, customer testimonials, or credentials.
* Avoid complex features that add maintenance without improving the experience.
Make it easy to add new manufacturer profiles and catalogs later by editing index.html directly.

14. Scope for the First Version
Build the three principal pages and the contact modal.
The first version should establish the information architecture, visual identity, page layouts, responsive behavior, and representative content patterns.
Do not attempt to build a comprehensive manufacturer database or elaborate sourcing platform.
Where real content or assets are unavailable, use clearly labeled placeholders rather than fabricating details.
The goal is a convincing, usable first version that can be evaluated and refined quickly.
