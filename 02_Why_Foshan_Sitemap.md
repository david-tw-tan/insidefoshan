2. Opening Section — Why Foshan?
Purpose
Answer the visitor's basic question quickly: why do people interested in furniture explore Foshan?
Content
A concise introduction describing Foshan as an important furniture-manufacturing and furniture-trading destination in China.
Explain that the attraction is not a single showroom or one type of product. It is the opportunity to explore a broad ecosystem of manufacturers, showrooms, and furniture categories.
Avoid exaggerated claims, unsupported statistics, and generic statements about Foshan being the world's best or cheapest source of furniture.

3. A Broad Furniture Ecosystem
Purpose
Explain the variety visitors may encounter.
Content themes
* Manufacturers covering multiple rooms and furniture categories.
* Specialists focused on particular products.
* Showrooms that allow visitors to see a broad range of furniture in person.
* Different design approaches, materials, finishes, quality levels, and price positions.
* Manufacturers operating under different business models.
Emphasize that Foshan is not one uniform market. The visitor's experience depends on which manufacturers, showrooms, and products they explore.

4. Designer-Inspired Furniture and Residential Projects
Purpose
Connect Foshan's furniture ecosystem to the needs of residential homeowners.
Content themes
* Visitors may find furniture in styles inspired by established international design traditions.
* Different manufacturers may offer different interpretations of similar design directions.
* Exploring furniture in person can help visitors assess materials, proportions, finishes, and construction.
* Product appearance alone does not establish quality; materials, workmanship, specifications, and suitability also matter.
Keep the discussion practical and measured.
Do not imply that every product is an original designer piece, an authorized design, or equivalent to a particular branded product.

5. Quality, Variety, and Price Positioning
Purpose
Help visitors understand the range of the market without suggesting that every part of it is represented on InsideFoshan.
Content themes
* Foshan serves a wide range of buyers and business models.
* Quality and price can vary substantially between manufacturers and products.
* Manufacturers may specialize in different materials, construction methods, finishes, and market segments.
* Buyers should evaluate individual products and manufacturers rather than assume that location alone guarantees quality or value.
Make clear that InsideFoshan deliberately focuses on a narrower selection of high-quality residential furniture manufacturers.
Avoid publishing specific price comparisons or statistics unless verified information is supplied.

6. Why the Market Can Be Difficult to Navigate
Purpose
Acknowledge the practical challenges faced by international visitors.
Content themes
* The market is large and geographically dispersed.
* Many manufacturers and showrooms may be unfamiliar to overseas buyers.
* Product ranges, specifications, and communication practices can vary.
* Catalogs do not always provide a complete picture of actual products.
* Identifying manufacturers that fit a particular project's quality expectations can take time.
The tone should be informative, not alarmist. Do not imply that visitors cannot navigate the market without an agent.

7. How InsideFoshan Helps
Purpose
Explain the website's specific role.
Content themes
* It presents a curated selection rather than attempting to list every manufacturer.
* It organizes information into broad-range and specialist categories.
* It provides selected catalogs and real showroom imagery where useful.
* It helps visitors orient themselves before deciding what to explore next.
Reinforce that the website is an information resource. Visitors may use it independently.

Content and Design Rules
* Keep the page informative rather than promotional.
* Answer the main question early.
* Use concise sections with descriptive headings.
* Avoid turning the page into a history of Foshan or an exhaustive account of the furniture industry.
* Avoid unsupported claims, statistics, and superlatives.
* Distinguish the wider Foshan market from InsideFoshan's curated selection.
* Use genuine contextual photography only when it adds value.
* Do not use generic stock imagery merely to fill space.
* Link naturally to Furniture Catalogs and How to Shop.
