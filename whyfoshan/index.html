Why Foshan

China’s furniture capital—and why homeowners are paying attention.

China’s Furniture Capital

Foshan isn’t just a place that makes furniture. It’s a place where the furniture business has changed dramatically—and where homeowners can now shop in ways that would have been impossible a few years ago.

For decades, much of Foshan’s furniture industry focused on supplying wholesale (B2B) customers and trade buyers. But things began to change, especially after 2020.

01

From B2B to Direct-to-Consumer

The shift toward direct-to-consumer (DTC) sales began before the COVID pandemic and exploded in the years that followed. Chinese consumers emerged from lockdown ready to spend on their homes, just as social media commerce and factory-direct e-commerce took off. Seemingly overnight, Foshan became a household name for Chinese homeowners furnishing an entire home over a weekend trip.

The impact on the industry was profound. Selling directly to demanding homeowners pushed Foshan manufacturers to raise their game—improving product quality and service, embracing the latest designer-inspired styles, and matching European makers’ standards.

International homeowners soon took notice. Today, more buyers from overseas are traveling to Foshan to see collections and buy factory-direct.

02

A Remarkable Concentration of Furniture

The first thing that stands out about the Foshan furniture industry is its scale. The quantity of high-end designer showrooms here is unmatched anywhere in the world.

Foshan was already home to the Louvre International Furniture Mall, China’s largest luxury furniture mall. But the story doesn’t end there. The direct-to-consumer boom encouraged manufacturers to build massive, multi-storey showrooms of their own—think IKEA size—showcasing their entire collections under one roof and serving as one-stop shops for buyers. Today, hundreds of standalone factory showrooms are spread across Foshan, offering a compelling alternative to furniture malls, especially for buyers furnishing an entire home.

From sofas and dining sets to beds, wardrobes, outdoor furniture, and custom cabinetry, buyers can find furniture for every room of the home here.

03

Designer-Inspired Furniture, Factory-Direct

Until recently, homeowners typically bought designer furniture through local luxury retailers. Foshan lets you buy directly from manufacturers, at lower prices—even after shipping, duties, taxes, and delivery.

But price is only part of the appeal. The right factories offer impressive quality and a broad range of styles and products. High-end Foshan factories also typically build to order, allowing buyers to customize materials, upholstery, colors, and more in ways that aren’t possible when buying through retail.

Foshan’s manufacturers are also aggressive in new product development. The very best ones set an annual release cycle around the Milan International Furniture Fair: they watch for emerging designs, order samples, and develop their own designer-inspired interpretations. The newest collections are available in Foshan before the year is out.

04

Not Every Foshan Factory Is Equal

For homeowners buying directly from Foshan, finding a reputable factory is essential—but not always easy. Foshan has tens of thousands of factories and suppliers. Knowing who you’re dealing with is critical.

Not every showroom belongs to a factory. Some are retailers or aggregators sourcing from multiple manufacturers, while others are traditional factories focused primarily on wholesale customers. Even factories that accept smaller orders may subcontract production if those orders aren’t a priority.

The key is to understand what type of factory you’re buying from, whether it’s set up for direct-to-consumer sales, and what buyers and industry peers say about its reputation. Compare your options, ask the right questions, and do your due diligence.
